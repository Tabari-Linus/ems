Initial design-system scaffold (Slice 0.8). `@ems/tokens`: placeholder DTCG tokens (primitive,
semantic light/dark, button component) built with Style Dictionary into CSS, TS and JSON, with a
WCAG contrast gate. `@ems/icons`: `ui-icon` over a curated Lucide set. `@ems/ui`: global styles
(cascade layers, reset, base), `@ems/ui/button` and `@ems/ui/theme`.
