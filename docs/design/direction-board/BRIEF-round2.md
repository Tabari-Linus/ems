# Direction Board — round 2 brief

Round 1 (A Atelier, B Kente Modern, C Precision) was rejected: the owner found the designs off overall.
Round 2 follows the owner's stated taste exactly:

- **References:** Rippling and Deel (modern HR software: approachable, structured business UI).
- **Feel:** clean and minimal, light-first (dark mode still required and equally finished).
- **Accent:** blue / indigo.
- **No cultural motifs, no pattern decoration.** Neutral, global, professional, quietly modern.

Everything in `BRIEF.md` still applies (sample data, people, Kwame's profile, technical contract,
contrast, responsive rules, file size) EXCEPT the changes below.

## Changes from round 1

   1. **Sign in** — simple, calm, centred or left-aligned form; product mark; "Forgot password?" note
      (request goes to your manager). No hero imagery or pattern.
   2. **Home (employee view)** — new. Signed in as Ama Mensah (Engineering Manager). Greeting with

   3. **People directory** — grid/list toggle visible (show the direction's preferred default), search,
      filters (Office, Department, Status), count "9 people", all 9 people.
   4. **Employee profile** (Kwame, viewed by his manager) — **tabs**: Overview · Job · Personal ·
      Documents · Activity, with Overview active showing: header (avatar, name, position,
      employee no. with copy, status, reports to Ama Mensah), work details, private details with the
      "Visible to you, your manager and HR" marker, masked ID documents, and the pending
      password-reset request with Approve / Decline.
2. **Foundations strip** as before (palette light + dark with roles and OKLCH values, type specimen,
   radius/elevation, buttons, input with hint + error, status pills, avatar, tag).
3. **Fonts:** do not use Inter, Space Grotesk, Syne, Manrope, Onest, Figtree or Bricolage Grotesque
   (round 1 / generic). Pick from Google Fonts per the direction brief.
4. **Blue is allowed and expected** (the owner chose it). Make it considered: a specific hue and
   chroma, neutrals tinted toward it, a lighter/brighter tuned variant for dark mode. No gradients.
5. Department colour, if used at all, is subtle (small dots or soft avatar tints) — never stripes or patterns.
